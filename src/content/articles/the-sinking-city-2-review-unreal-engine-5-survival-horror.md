With *The Sinking City 2*, Ukrainian developer Frogwares has executed a decisive genre pivot. Where the 2019 original leaned heavily into slow-paced detective deduction and open-world investigations, the sequel strips away the filler to deliver a focused, claustrophobic **third-person survival horror experience** built inside **Unreal Engine 5**.

Trading the foggy streets of Oakmont for a semi-submerged, decay-ridden 1920s Arkham, *The Sinking City 2* successfully channels the tense, resource-strapped design of classic survival horror franchises while maintaining its signature cosmic dread.

---

## 🌊 Setting & Narrative: Drowned Streets of Arkham

Taking place in a standalone storyline separate from the original, players step into the boots of **Calvin Rafferty**. Following an occult ritual gone wrong, Rafferty's partner, Faye, is trapped in a supernatural coma, pulling him into the drowned, labyrinthine district of Arkham to reverse the affliction.

| Narrative Element | The Sinking City (2019) | The Sinking City 2 (2026) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Protagonist** | Charles Reed (Private Eye) | **Calvin Rafferty** |
| **Primary Location** | Oakmont, Massachusetts | **Arkham (Drowned Districts)** |
| **Core Tone** | Investigative Noir / Deduction | **Tight Survival Horror / Resource Management** |
| **Structure** | Open-world vehicle transit | **Interconnected Metroidvania-style Hubs** |

Arkham feels immediate and threatening. Rather than an oversized, empty city map, Frogwares designed dense, multi-level tenement blocks, waterlogged cellars, and dilapidated manors linked by unlockable shortcuts and one-way gates.

---

## 🔫 Combat & Gameplay: The Survival Horror Overhaul

The biggest mechanical leap in *The Sinking City 2* is its moment-to-moment combat and pacing. Frogwares replaced clunky gunplay with deliberate, weighty third-person shooting that rewards patience and precise shot placement:

* **Grid-Based Inventory Management:** Storage space is strictly limited. Balancing revolver ammunition, shotgun shells, bandages, and crafting chemicals forces constant tactical decision-making before exploring new wings.
* **Slither & Aberration Enemy Variants:** Mutated husks and grotesque aquatic monstrosities react dynamically to dismemberment and stagger damage. Headshots are difficult in dark corridors, making environmental hazards and defensive dodges essential.
* **Optional Investigation Loops:** While detective deduction is no longer mandatory to advance the main narrative, exploring optional crime scenes and environmental notes rewards players with weapon upgrade parts, rare ammunition caches, and key lore.

---

## 🖥️ Technical Breakdown: Unreal Engine 5 Performance

Utilizing **Lumen global illumination** and **Nanite geometry**, the game excels in environmental rendering:

* **Lighting & Reflections:** Submerged floors dynamically reflect swinging oil lanterns and flashes of lightning, heightening tension in pitch-black interiors.
* **Console Performance:** On PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X, the title offers a stable **Performance Mode (Target 60 FPS at dynamic 1440p)** alongside a **Quality Mode (Native 4K at 30 FPS with ray-traced shadows)**.
* **PC Optimization:** Supports DLSS 3.7, FSR 3.1, and Intel XeSS upscaling out of the box, allowing mid-range cards like the RTX 3060/4060 to achieve consistent 60+ FPS at 1080p/1440p with High settings.

## ⚖️ The Verdict

Frogwares has successfully transformed *The Sinking City* from an ambitious investigative experiment into a top-tier survival horror entry. The tight Metroidvania-inspired level design, oppressive UE5 presentation, and satisfyingly tense combat make *The Sinking City 2* an easy recommendation for fans of survival horror and Lovecraftian fiction alike.
