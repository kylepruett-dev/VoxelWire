Led by *The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt* director Konrad Tomaszkiewicz, newly formed studio **Rebel Wolves** and publisher Bandai Namco have pulled back the curtain on **The Blood of Dawnwalker**. 

Set in the 14th-century fictional region of Vale Sangora in the Carpathian Mountains during the Black Death, this dark fantasy narrative sandbox RPG introduces an unconventional structure: an open world governed by a consequential **30-day narrative clock** and a dual-form combat system.

---

## 🩸 The Dual Gameplay Loop: Human by Day, Vampire by Night

Players assume the role of **Coen**, a young man transformed into a "Dawnwalker"—a hybrid being walking the knife's edge between humanity and vampirism. Combat mechanics, NPC reactivity, and movement options shift completely based on the in-game sun cycle:

| Mechanics Pillar | Daytime State (Human) | Nighttime State (Vampire) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Primary Combat** | Deliberate historical swordplay, parries & holy magic | Superhuman claw strikes, gravity-defying leaps & blood fury |
| **Social Reactivity** | Enter medieval settlements, barter & speak with human NPCs | Towns enter total lockdown; guards and inquisitors attack on sight |
| **Traversal** | Standard riding & on-foot sprint exploration | High-speed roof vaulting, shadow dashes & vertical wall traversal |
| **Vulnerabilities** | Standard damage thresholds & limited stamina pools | Intense vulnerability to silver, fire, and sun exposure |

---

## ⏳ The 30-Day Narrative Sandbox: How the Clock Works

Unlike traditional RPGs where main quests wait indefinitely for the player, *The Blood of Dawnwalker* gives players 30 days and nights to save Coen's captured family from the vampiric feudal lord, Brencis:

* **Plot Actions Advance Time:** Exploring the open world, discovering lore, and fighting wandering beasts **do not** advance the clock. Time moves forward only when accepting and executing major narrative decisions and story quests.
* **The 8-Segment Day:** Each day and night cycle is divided into eight discrete time segments. Main quests consume one or more segments depending on their scope.
* **Deliberate Scarcity:** There are far more story quests in Vale Sangora than there are available time segments across 30 days. Players are forced to choose which factions to aid, which allies to recruit, and which leads to abandon.
* **No Sudden "Game Over":** Running out of the 30 days does not instantly trigger a game-over screen; instead, it fundamentally shifts the world state into a high-stakes final reckoning where your unpreparedness directly impacts who survives.

## 🖥️ Unreal Engine 5 Tech & System Requirements

Built natively on Unreal Engine 5, Rebel Wolves is utilizing Lumen lighting to create stark contrasts between gloomy medieval taverns and pitch-black Carpathian pine forests:

### Minimum PC Requirements (1080p / 30 FPS / Low)
* **OS:** Windows 10/11 (64-bit with DirectX 12)
* **Processor:** Intel Core i7-8700K or AMD Ryzen 7 3700X
* **Memory:** 16 GB RAM
* **Graphics:** NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3050 / GTX 1070 or AMD Radeon RX Vega 56
* **Storage:** 60 GB SSD

### Recommended PC Requirements (1080p / 60 FPS / High)
* **Processor:** Intel Core i5-13600 or AMD Ryzen 9 7900X
* **Memory:** 16 GB RAM
* **Graphics:** NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4070 / RTX 5060 or AMD Radeon RX 6800 XT
* **Storage:** 60 GB SSD

## 🗓️ Release Date & Editions

*The Blood of Dawnwalker* officially launches on **September 3, 2026**, for PC (Steam), PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X|S. 

In addition to the standard edition ($69.99), the digital **Eclipse Edition** includes the official orchestral score by Mikolai Stroinski (*The Witcher 3*) and a digital world compendium comic book detailing the vampire lords of Vale Sangora.
