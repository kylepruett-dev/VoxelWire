Microsoft is making its presence felt in Cologne, Germany for Gamescom 2026. Celebrating the 25th anniversary of the Xbox brand, the publisher is taking over Hall 7 at the Koelnmesse with 140 hands-on gaming stations, a dedicated *Fable* combat theater, and two massive live developer broadcasts scheduled across the week.

Whether you are attending the show floor in person or watching the live streams from home, here is the complete breakdown of Xbox’s Gamescom 2026 showcase schedule, confirmed playable games, and stream start times.

---

## Xbox Gamescom 2026 Live Broadcast Schedule

For fans watching remotely, Xbox is hosting two dedicated live broadcasts straight from their show floor booth stage on **Wednesday, August 26, and Thursday, August 27**.

| Broadcast Day | Pacific (PDT) | Eastern (EDT) | UK (BST) | Central Europe (CEST) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Day 1: Wednesday, August 26** | 7:00 AM | 10:00 AM | 3:00 PM | 4:00 PM |
| **Day 2: Thursday, August 27** | 7:00 AM | 10:00 AM | 3:00 PM | 4:00 PM |

Both broadcasts will feature deep-dive gameplay walk-throughs, developer interviews, new trailers, and special guest appearances. Streams will air live across the official **Xbox YouTube**, **Twitch**, and **Facebook Gaming** channels with multi-language and accessibility audio descriptions.

---

## Confirmed First-Party Lineup: What’s Playable?

Xbox Game Studios is bringing several of its most anticipated first-party tentpoles directly to the show floor for hands-on impressions:

* **Fable (Playground Games):** Features a dedicated live demo theater offering an extended look at the action-RPG’s overhauled melee, magic systems, and British humor.
* **Gears of War: E-Day (The Coalition):** Playable hands-on stations showcasing the prequel’s gritty return to Emergence Day powered by Unreal Engine 5.
* **Minecraft Dungeons II (Mojang / Double Eleven):** First-ever public hands-on demo for the anticipated co-op dungeon-crawling sequel.
* **Forza Horizon 6:** Dedicated sim-rig stations demonstrating next-gen physics and dynamic weather.

---

## Major Third-Party & Partner Showcases

Alongside its first-party studios, the Xbox Hall 7 booth features 25+ upcoming titles from third-party partners and ID@Xbox developers:

* **Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 (Infinity Ward / Activision)** – Multiplayer hands-on stations.
* **Alien: Isolation 2 (Creative Assembly / Sega)** – Exclusive behind-closed-doors preview.
* **Metro 2039 (4A Games / Deep Silver)** – High-fidelity gameplay stations running on Series X hardware.
* **Persona 4 Revival (Atlus / Sega)** – Hands-on playable vertical slice.
* **Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis (Crystal Dynamics)** – Cinematic preview and combat breakdown.

---

## Booth Accessibility and FanFest 2026

Microsoft continues to set the standard for convention accessibility at this year’s booth (Hall 7, Booth A-0.61):

* **Sensory & Mobility Support:** Fully wheelchair-accessible demo pods, adjustable-height desks, Xbox Adaptive Controllers, and a quiet sensory room.
* **Sign Language Interpretation:** Live ASL, BSL, and DGS (German Sign Language) interpreters on-site from 12:00 PM to 5:00 PM daily.
* **Xbox FanFest:** Returning in-person on Wednesday evening, August 26, from 8:00 PM to 11:00 PM CEST for registered badge holders.
