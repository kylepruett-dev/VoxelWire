Cold Iron Studios is heading back into the bug hunt with **Aliens: Fireteam Elite 2**, launching globally on **Tuesday, August 25, 2026**. 

Building upon the third-person survival shooter formula of the 2021 original, the sequel expands squad dynamics with new Marine combat specializations, enhanced Xenomorph swarm intelligence, and a branching cooperative campaign set across deep-space Weyland-Yutani research outposts.

Here is the complete breakdown of launch dates, new gameplay mechanics, full crossplay support, and PC system requirements.

---

## Release Date & Platforms

*Aliens: Fireteam Elite 2* officially releases on **August 25, 2026**, for:
* **PlayStation 5**
* **Xbox Series X | S**
* **PC (Steam & Epic Games Store)**

---

## Full Crossplay & Matchmaking Support

Cold Iron Studios has confirmed that **full cross-platform play and cross-progression** will be active at launch across all platforms. 

Squads of up to three players can seamlessly team up across PC, PS5, and Xbox. Solo players will once again have access to customizable synthetic squadmates (Alpha and Beta) with improved tactical AI behavior and skill command wheels.

## Expanded Xenomorph Threat Matrix

*Fireteam Elite 2* introduces dynamic swarm pathing where Xenomorphs scale walls, ceilings, and ventilation shafts without scripted spawn patterns. New elite variants include:

* **Acid Spitter Stalker:** Uses thermal cloaking in dark corridors before firing long-range corrosive acid bursts.
* **Crusher Juggernaut:** Heavily armored quadruped variant that charges defensive barricades, requiring focused weak-point fire to bring down.
* **Synoid Infiltrator:** Corrupted Weyland-Yutani combat androids armed with pulse rifles and flash grenades, demanding tactical cover-to-cover combat.
