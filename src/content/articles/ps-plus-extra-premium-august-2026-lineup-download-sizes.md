Sony Interactive Entertainment has officially rolled out the **PlayStation Plus Extra and Premium Game Catalog lineup for August 2026**. 

Headlined by Arrowhead’s co-op shooter *Helldivers 2* and Warhorse Studios' medieval RPG *Kingdom Come: Deliverance 2*, this month's catalog update adds major AAA campaigns alongside classic retro titles for Premium tier members.

## 🕹️ PS Plus Premium Classics & Remasters

PlayStation Plus Premium tier members also gain access to two legacy titles enhanced with custom up-rendering, rewind features, and quick-save states:

* **Onimusha: Dawn of Dreams (PS2 Classic / PS4 & PS5):** Capcom’s four-character action-adventure with updated trophy support and visual resolution scaling.
* **Disney’s Atlantis: The Lost Empire (PS1 Classic / PS4 & PS5):** The classic platformer featuring custom display filters and CRT screen simulation modes.

## 🔍 Standout Highlights: What to Download First

### 1. Helldivers 2 (PS5)
Arrowhead's live-service hit makes its subscription service debut alongside its latest update and the *Warhammer 40,000* crossover Warbond. With cross-play enabled across PC and console, squad queues are currently operating at peak capacity.

### 2. Kingdom Come: Deliverance 2 (PS5)
Henry’s story continues across 15th-century Bohemia. Delivering historical sword combat, reactive NPC routines, and high-fidelity environmental rendering on current-gen hardware, this is the largest storage commitment of the month at **88.2 GB**.

### 3. Hell is Us (PS5)
A non-linear, dark fantasy action title built around exploration without explicit quest markers or compass waypoints. If you enjoy environmental problem-solving and deliberate melee pacing, it provides a distinct alternative to standard open-world formulas.
