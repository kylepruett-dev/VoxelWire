If you are looking to upgrade your battle station on a budget, here are the top 5 best 1440p gaming monitors available right now under the $250 mark.

## 1. Gigabyte M27Q (Rev 2.0) – The Undisputed Best Overall

![Gigabyte M27Q 27-inch 1440p Gaming Monitor](/images/gigabyte-m27q-monitor.jpg)

The Gigabyte M27Q continues to dominate the sub-$250 category. Featuring a 27-inch Super Speed IPS panel running at 170Hz, it delivers exceptional motion clarity with negligible overshoot or ghosting.

* **Key Strengths:** Built-in KVM switch (control two PCs with one keyboard/mouse), 95% DCI-P3 wide color gamut, and solid factory calibration.
* **Drawback:** The stand lacks swivel adjustment, though it includes a standard 100x100 VESA mount.

---

## 2. Dell G2724D – Premium Build Quality and Color Accuracy

![Dell G2724D 27-inch Fast IPS Gaming Monitor](/images/dell-g2724d-monitor.jpg)

Dell's entry-level gaming monitor punches well above its price class with a standout fully ergonomic stand (height, tilt, swivel, and pivot) and an ultra-clean aesthetic.

* **Key Strengths:** Outstanding out-of-the-box sRGB clamp mode for creators, official NVIDIA G-Sync Compatible certification, and zero backlight bleed issues common in budget IPS displays.
* **Drawback:** Lacks built-in audio or dedicated USB passthrough ports.

---

## 3. AOC Q27G3XMN – The Budget HDR and Mini-LED King

![AOC Q27G3XMN 27-inch Mini-LED Gaming Monitor](/images/aoc-q27g3xmn-monitor.jpg)

If you play cinematic single-player titles like *Cyberpunk 2077* or *Alan Wake 2*, traditional IPS panels can look washed out in dark scenes. The AOC Q27G3XMN solves this by packing true Mini-LED local dimming into a sub-$250 price point.

* **Key Strengths:** True HDR performance with deep blacks, high peak brightness over 1000 nits, and 180Hz refresh rate.
* **Drawback:** VA panel viewing angles are narrower than IPS, and dark-level pixel transitions can show minor smearing in competitive shooters.

---

## 4. ASUS TUF Gaming VG27AQ1A – Rock-Solid Motion Clarity

![ASUS TUF Gaming VG27AQ1A 27-inch Gaming Monitor](/images/asus-tuf-vg27aq1a-monitor.jpg)

The VG27AQ1A features ASUS's proprietary ELMB Sync (Extreme Low Motion Blur), allowing back-light strobing to run simultaneously with Variable Refresh Rate (G-Sync/FreeSync).

* **Key Strengths:** Eliminates motion blur entirely in fast-paced competitive esports titles like *Apex Legends* and *Valorant*.
* **Drawback:** The stock stand is tilt-only, so budget a VESA desk arm for height adjustment.

---

## 5. Koorui 27" Fast IPS – The Best Sub-$180 Option

![Koorui 27E1Q 27-inch Fast IPS Gaming Monitor](/images/koorui-27e1q-monitor.jpg)

For shoppers strictly watching their wallet, Koorui delivers a genuine Fast IPS 1440p 170Hz experience often on sale between $160 and $180.

* **Key Strengths:** Unbeatable price-to-performance ratio with vibrant colors and FreeSync support.
* **Drawback:** Basic plastic construction and limited on-screen display (OSD) controls.

---

## Buying Advice: What to Prioritize in 2026

* **Avoid TN Panels:** Fast IPS and modern VA have rendered TN panels obsolete; there is no reason to sacrifice color quality for response times anymore.
* **Check for DisplayPort 1.4:** To run 1440p at 165Hz+ with 10-bit color without chroma subsampling, always connect via DisplayPort rather than older HDMI 2.0 cables.
* **Mounting:** If you choose a monitor with a basic tilt-only stand, look for a $25–$30 single-monitor VESA desk arm to reclaim desk space and get ideal eye-level ergonomics.
