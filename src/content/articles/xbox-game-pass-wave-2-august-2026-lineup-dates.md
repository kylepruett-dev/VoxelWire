Microsoft has revealed the official **Xbox Game Pass Wave 2 lineup for late August 2026**, adding high-profile Day One blockbusters, dark action RPGs, and indie standouts across Xbox Series X|S, PC, and Xbox Cloud Gaming.

Headlined by Asobo Studio's Day One launch of *Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy* and the atmospheric action title *Vapor World: Over The Mind*, this update marks one of the strongest subscription updates of late summer.

## 🔍 Standout Highlights: What to Pre-Install

### 1. Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy (Day One — August 27)
Asobo Studio returns with a bold evolution of its dark historical universe. Shifting toward deeper real-time combat, expanded weapon crafting, and dynamic swarm physics, *Resonance* represents Microsoft’s marquee Day One Game Pass addition for late August.

### 2. Vapor World: Over The Mind (August 19)
A surreal, hand-drawn action-adventure featuring dynamic parry mechanics and psychological horror exploration. Built with layered 2D perspective and fluid combat encounters, it is available across Cloud, Console, and PC.

### 3. Blood Dungeon (August 25)
A fast-paced dungeon crawler blending rogue-lite weapon progression with high-intensity isometric melee combat, designed for quick replayability and class experimentation.

## ⚠️ Games Leaving Xbox Game Pass (August 31, 2026)

The following titles will rotate out of the service on **August 31, 2026**. Subscribers can utilize their 20% membership discount to purchase and retain access:

* *Atomic Heart* (Cloud, Console, PC)
* *Guilty Gear -Strive-* (Cloud, Console, PC)
* *Shadow Warrior 3: Definitive Edition* (Cloud, Console, PC)
* *Sea of Stars* (Cloud, Console, PC)
