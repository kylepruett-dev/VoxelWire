Gamescom 2026 in Cologne, Germany, has officially sold out its available exhibition space for the first time in event history. Running from **August 25 through August 30, 2026**, the world's largest public gaming expo is headlined by Geoff Keighley’s **Opening Night Live (ONL)** and unprecedented hands-on show floors from Xbox, Nintendo, Capcom, CD Projekt RED, and Konami.

Whether you are tuning into the live broadcasts or following the breaking show-floor impressions, here is the complete, confirmed breakdown of times, streaming links, playable demos, and major reveals.

## 🎮 Confirmed Playable Show Floor Demos

For attendees on the show floor in Cologne, several highly anticipated AAA and mid-tier titles will be publicly playable for the first time:

* **Gears of War: E-Day (The Coalition / Xbox Game Studios):** Playable combat build showcasing Marcus Fenix and Dom Santiago during the Emergence Day invasion on Unreal Engine 5.
* **Alien: Isolation 2 (Creative Assembly):** First public hands-on survival horror demo featuring upgraded dynamic Xenomorph AI behavior.
* **Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 (Activision):** Multiplayer stations offering early hands-on rounds ahead of the upcoming open beta cycle.
* **Metro 2039 (4A Games):** Post-apocalyptic survival shooter demo highlighting surface exploration mechanics and upgraded weapon maintenance.
* **Fable (Playground Games):** Open-world fantasy gameplay demonstration focusing on real-time combat and Albion’s moral reactivity systems.
* **Persona 4 Revival (Atlus / Sega):** High-definition remake hands-on station demonstrating updated turn-based dungeon combat and visual overhauls.
* **007 First Light (IO Interactive):** Playable stealth-action showcase running on Nintendo Switch 2 and current-gen consoles.

## 🌟 Major Confirmed Premieres at Opening Night Live

Geoff Keighley and publisher partners have locked in several major title reveals and deep-dive looks for the ONL broadcast:

### 1. The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt – Songs of the Past (CD Projekt RED)
CD Projekt RED will reveal the first extensive gameplay look at *Songs of the Past*, an expansive new story addition taking players back into Geralt’s earlier timeline with enhanced next-gen combat mechanics.

### 2. Crimson Desert & DUNE: Awakening
Pearl Abyss and Funcom will showcase dedicated gameplay updates. *Crimson Desert* will feature extended single-player boss combat sequences, while *Dune: Awakening* highlights large-scale Arrakis multiplayer survival mechanics.

### 3. Xbox Mega-Booth (140 Stations, 25 Games)
Microsoft’s presence dominates Hall 8 with over 140 gaming stations. In addition to *Fable* and *Gears of War: E-Day*, Xbox is featuring hands-on stations for *Minecraft Dungeons II*, *Stranger Than Heaven*, and third-party partner reveals.
