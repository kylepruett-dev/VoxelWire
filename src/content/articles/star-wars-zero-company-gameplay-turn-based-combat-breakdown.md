Developed by **Bit Reactor** (a studio composed of veteran *XCOM* and tactical RPG developers) in collaboration with Respawn and Lucasfilm Games, *Star Wars Zero Company* is bringing grid-based tactical strategy to the Star Wars universe. 

Set during the twilight of the Clone Wars, the single-player campaign places players in command of **Hawks**, a disillusioned former Republic officer leading an unconventional mercenary outfit known as **Zero Company**.

---

## 🎯 The Tactical Core: Cover, Action Points, and Synergy

*Zero Company* delivers deliberate, turn-based squad encounters where terrain destructibility, elevation, and unit positioning dictate mission outcomes:

| Tactical Pillar | Mechanics & Design |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Action Economy** | Two-action point system per operative (Move, Fire, Overwatch, Class Ability). |
| **Cover & Flanking** | Dynamic destructive cover (Low/High) that degrades under heavy blaster fire and thermal detonators. |
| **Cross-Unit Synergies** | Chaining abilities—such as pinning targets with Heavy Repeaters while Vanguard units flank—triggers critical damage multipliers. |
| **Morale & Suppression** | Intense suppression fire pins enemy droids and bounty hunters, reducing their turn accuracy and movement range. |

---

## 👥 Squad Management: Permadeath and Custom Recruitment

Between operations, survival depends on how you recruit, gear, and manage your roster:

* **Authored Specialists & Custom Recruits:** The roster blends key story companions with randomly generated mercenary recruits across multiple galactic factions (former Clones, Trandoshan hunters, Mandalorian outcasts, and combat droids).
* **Consequential Permadeath:** Fallen squad members during standard operations are permanently lost unless stabilized before extraction, forcing players to train redundant squad roles.
* **Class Progression Trees:** Four primary specializations (**Vanguard**, **Sharpshooter**, **Tech Infiltrator**, and **Heavy Demolitions**) feature divergent skill branches allowing tailored builds for stealth infiltration or frontal assault.

---

## 🛸 The Base of Operations: Deploying Informants

Between tactical skirmishes, players manage and expand a mobile headquarters to support ongoing galactic operations:

* **Galactic Intel Map:** Dispatching scouts and informants across underworld sectors uncovers high-value black-market weapons, specialized weapon mods, and time-sensitive rescue contracts.
* **Workbench Upgrades:** Fabricate customized blaster modifications, heat-sink upgrades, and specialized armor plating to counter specific enemy types (e.g., EMP shielding for heavy droid garrisons).
* **Synergy Bonds:** Pairing squad members on repeated deployments builds mutual battlefield trust, unlocking passive combo abilities and defensive reaction perks.

## 🖥️ Platform Specs & Editions

* **Target Platforms:** PC (Steam), PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X|S.
* **Performance Targets:** Native 4K at 60 FPS on current-gen consoles, with fully customizable keybindings and native controller grid support on PC.
* **Editions:** Standard Edition ($49.99 PC / $59.99 Console) and Deluxe Edition ($59.99 PC / $69.99 Console) featuring exclusive Clone Wars legacy armor sets and weapon themes.
