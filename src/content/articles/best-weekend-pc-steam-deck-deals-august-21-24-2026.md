## Quick Buyer's Tip for Steam Deck Optimization

When picking up older AAA titles like *Sleeping Dogs* or *Just Cause 3* on sale:
1. Set the **SteamOS Frame Limit to 45 FPS / 90Hz** (OLED) or **40 FPS / 40Hz** (LCD).
2. Cap the **TDP to 9W–10W** in the Quick Access Menu.
3. This keeps your fan near-silent and stretches handheld battery life past **4.5 to 5 hours** of continuous play.
