Microsoft has officially revealed the second wave of titles joining the Xbox Game Pass catalog for late August through September 1, 2026. This mid-month drop brings 10 additional games spanning day-one indie breakouts, action roguelites, and the highly anticipated day-one release of *Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy*.

Whether you subscribe to Game Pass Ultimate, PC Game Pass, or the newly expanded Game Pass Premium tier, here is the complete breakdown of every title arriving, release dates, supported platforms, and the games leaving the service soon.

## Headline Highlights for Late August

### 1. Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy (August 27 – Day One)
Asobo Studio and Focus Entertainment deliver a fresh take on the acclaimed medieval franchise. Setting aside the direct narrative of Amicia and Hugo, *Resonance* explores the broader dark folklore of the Macula with refined stealth-action mechanics and high-end Unreal Engine 5 visual fidelity on Series X|S and PC.

### 2. Blood Dungeon (August 25)
From the creative minds behind *Nidhogg*, *Blood Dungeon* delivers a frenetic, retro-inspired mash-up blending hyper-fast 2D platforming, top-down arcade survival, and intense procedural combat arenas.

### 3. Vapor World: Over The Mind (Game Preview – Out Now)
A moody, narrative-driven 2D action-adventure featuring deflection-heavy, Souls-inspired combat and surreal hand-painted environments exploring the inner psyche.

## Games Leaving Xbox Game Pass on August 31, 2026

As new titles enter the library, five games will depart the catalog at the end of the month. Members can use their standard **20% membership discount** to purchase and keep these titles before they leave:

* *The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt* (Console, PC, Cloud)
* *Sea of Stars* (Console, PC, Cloud)
* *Atomic Heart* (Console, PC, Cloud)
* *Guilty Gear -Strive-* (Console, PC, Cloud)
* *Shadow Warrior 3: Definitive Edition* (Console, PC, Cloud)
