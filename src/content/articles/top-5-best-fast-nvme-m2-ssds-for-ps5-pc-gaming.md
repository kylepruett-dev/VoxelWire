Whether you need to meet the PS5's strict 5,500 MB/s speed threshold or want top-tier DirectStorage loading speeds on Windows 11, here are the top 5 budget-friendly NVMe SSDs to buy right now.

## 1. WD_BLACK SN850X (Best Overall for PS5)

The **WD_BLACK SN850X** remains the gold standard for drop-in PlayStation 5 upgrades and enthusiast gaming desktops. 

* **Read Speed:** Up to 7,300 MB/s
* **Key Advantage:** Built-in low-profile aluminum heatsink fits directly into the PS5 M.2 expansion bay without needing third-party thermal pads.
* **Verdict:** The most reliable plug-and-play choice with an exceptional 5-year warranty and top-tier random read IOPS for cutting down open-world load screens.

---

## 2. Crucial T500 (Best Performance & Power Efficiency)

Powered by 232-layer TLC NAND flash and a dedicated LPDDR4 DRAM cache, the **Crucial T500** maximizes PCIe 4.0 bandwidth to deliver near-instantaneous DirectStorage asset streaming on PC.

* **Read Speed:** Up to 7,400 MB/s
* **Key Advantage:** Industry-leading power efficiency, running significantly cooler than older Gen4 controllers.
* **Verdict:** The ideal pick for tight ITX PC cases and handheld eGPU docks where thermal management is critical.

---

## 3. Lexar NM790 (Best Value 2TB Capacity Pick)

If your primary goal is raw capacity per dollar without sacrificing speed, the **Lexar NM790** uses the Maxio MAP1602 DRAM-less controller with Host Memory Buffer (HMB) 3.0 technology to punch well above its weight class.

* **Read Speed:** Up to 7,400 MB/s
* **Key Advantage:** Frequently drops under $100 for a massive **2TB model**, making it the cheapest way to double your game library storage.
* **Verdict:** Fully compliant with PS5 specifications while delivering cool operating temperatures even under sustained write loads.

---

## 4. Samsung 990 EVO Plus (Best Hybrid Interface)

Samsung’s updated **990 EVO Plus** provides unique hybrid lane support, operating on either PCIe 4.0 x4 or PCIe 5.0 x2 configurations.

* **Read Speed:** Up to 7,250 MB/s
* **Key Advantage:** Versatile lane routing provides future-proof compatibility with newer budget motherboards running dedicated Gen5 x2 slots.
* **Verdict:** A stellar all-around drive backed by Samsung's reliable Magician SSD software for firmware updates and health tracking.

---

## 5. Teamgroup MP44 (Best Ultra-Budget Pick)

For PC builders stretching every dollar, the **Teamgroup MP44** delivers top-tier sequential speeds with a super-thin graphene thermal label that installs easily under standard motherboard heat spreaders.

* **Read Speed:** Up to 7,400 MB/s
* **Key Advantage:** Aggressive pricing often sitting under $70 for 1TB, leaving more room in your budget for GPU or RAM upgrades.
* **Verdict:** The unmatched price-to-performance champion for custom desktop builds.

---

## Buyer's Checklist: Installing in a PlayStation 5

Before sealing your drive into your console, ensure:
1. **Speed Requirement:** Your drive meets or exceeds Sony's recommended **5,500 MB/s read speed** (all 5 drives above comfortably surpass 7,200 MB/s).
2. **Heatsink Clearance:** The total height of the drive plus heatsink must not exceed **11.25 mm** to fit properly under the metal expansion slot cover.
3. **Firmware Updates:** If you buy a drive without a factory heatsink, install an inexpensive 8mm aluminum M.2 heatsink with silicone thermal pads.
