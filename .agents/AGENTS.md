# Workspace Rules for VoxelWire

## Article Image Guidelines

1. **Authentic Game Screenshots**:
   - For all future articles, ALWAYS use authentic in-game screenshots or official press key art directly from Steam Store CDN API or publisher press hubs for the game being covered.
   - Do NOT use generic or synthetic stock images.

2. **VoxelWire Brand Overlay**:
   - Maintain the signature VoxelWire brand badge overlay (`/favicon.svg` + `VoxelWire` styling) on article header images and hero banners to preserve brand consistency and visual polish across the site.
